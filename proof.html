Straight answers

Is it 100% private?

No, and we'd be lying if we said so. Your memory, chats and files stay on the box. When a task needs a frontier model, a filtered slice of that request goes to the provider you chose. Here's exactly what leaves.

Do I need a cloud subscription?

For frontier-level answers, yes: your own OpenAI (Codex) or OpenRouter account. The box also works on its own for everyday things like finding, remembering and reminding, with its on-device model.

How is this different from ChatGPT, Grok or Claude?

Those are brilliant brains, and you can still use them, through the box. The difference is who keeps the mind: with them, the memory of you lives on their servers and works only with their model. With inteligentia it lives on your desk and works with any of them.

Can I bring my ChatGPT and Claude history?

Yes. Export your conversations from OpenAI or Anthropic (both offer a download), hand the file to your agent, and it imports them. Years of context, moved onto your desk.

How do I talk to it?

On Telegram, or another chat app you prefer, on your phone or laptop. Your agent is a contact; messages go to the box on your desk.

Couldn't I build this myself?

If you enjoy compiling drivers, wiring API keys and babysitting a server, absolutely, and the open-source core is there for you. inteligentia is for people who'd rather plug in a beautiful box and start talking.

What if inteligentia disappears?

Your memory and files are on a drive you own, and the agent at its core is open source. You're not locked in to the cloud vendors, and you're not locked in to us.
