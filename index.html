Privacy

What leaves your desk.

Most answers come from the box itself. When a question needs a bigger model, only the question travels, with names, numbers and addresses masked. The real values stay on the box and are put back into the answer there.

1 · You ask, on your desk

Draft a reply to Maria Popescu: she can pay invoice RO-2024-117 for €4,800 in two parts, by 15 December.

2 · What your cloud provider sees

Draft a reply to [PERSON_1]: she can pay invoice [INVOICE_1] for [AMOUNT_1] in two parts, by [DATE_1].

3 · What you get back

Dear Maria Popescu, thank you for your message. Paying €4,800 in two parts by 15 December works for me…

The names, numbers and dates are swapped for placeholders before anything leaves, and put back on the box when the answer returns. The provider never sees who you're writing to.

Always stays on the box
  • Your memory: everything it learns about you
  • Your chats, including imported history
  • Your files and their index
  • The real names, numbers and addresses
Can leave, filtered
  • A single question that needs a frontier model
  • Sent to the provider you picked, nobody else
  • Personal details replaced with placeholders, like [PERSON_1]

Not 100% private, and we won't pretend otherwise. Minimum exposure, by design, and no lock-in.

Questions

        
Before you reserve.

        
Something missing? Write to info@inteligentia.eu. A person answers.

      

      

        

          Do I need to be technical?
          
No. The box comes set up. You pair it with your chat app and start talking to it. Connecting your email, calendar and apps is something we do with you, not something you configure in a terminal.

        

        

          What does it cost to run?
          
The box is a one-time purchase. Everyday work runs on the box itself. For the questions that need a frontier model, you pay your chosen provider directly, and only for what you use. You can set a spending cap with your provider, so there are no surprises.

        

        

          Is it really private?
          
Your memory, chats and files stay on the box. When a question needs the cloud, only that question leaves, with names, numbers and addresses masked. It's not 100% private and we won't pretend it is, but it's the least exposure we know how to build. Read exactly what leaves, or test it yourself.

        

        

          Can it send emails or spend money on its own?
          
Not unless you let it. By default it drafts and you approve: emails, invoices and anything that leaves your name on it wait for your tap.

        

        

          Which chat apps does it work with?
          
Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, email and SMS. Most people start with Telegram, because it's the quickest to pair.

        

        

          What if inteligentia disappears?
          
Your box keeps working. The agent at its core is open source, your memory and files are on your desk, and you hold the accounts with your chat app and AI provider. Nothing depends on us staying around.

        

        

          What does reserving commit me to?
          
Nothing. It's free, with no card. It holds your place for the tier you pick, and you hear from us before orders open. You pay only if you decide to order, after seeing the full terms.
