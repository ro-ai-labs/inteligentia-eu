Hi. I'm your agent.

Not an app. Not someone else's cloud. A small box on your desk that runs your own AI agent.

You talk to it on Telegram, or another chat app you prefer. It remembers how you work, searches your contracts, notes and invoices, and keeps all of it on the box. When a question needs a frontier model, it borrows one from the provider you choose, after masking your personal details.

What it does

It takes the busywork off your plate.

Think of a sharp assistant who lives in your chat app. Ask in plain words, send a voice note, or give it a schedule. It does the job and comes back with the result.

Every morning, 8:00

Your day in one message

Today's meetings, the emails that need you and the invoices due this week, waiting before your coffee.

Before a call

Briefed on the client

Who they are, what you last agreed, what they still owe you and what's new at their company.

After a call

Voice note in, follow-up out

Talk for a minute on your way out. You get the notes, the to-dos and a follow-up email ready for you to send.

Your inbox

Email sorted, replies drafted

Your money

Invoices that don't slip

It drafts invoices from your hours, chases the unpaid ones with you and checks payments against your spreadsheet.

In the background

It keeps watch for you

Competitor news, new tenders, a price drop, a page that changes. It only pings you when something actually happened.

Research

Market scans, competitor comparisons, lead lists. Every claim comes with a link, so you can check it.

Over time

Walk it through your month-end once. It saves the steps, and next time “do the month-end” is enough.

Privacy

What leaves your desk.

Most answers come from the box itself. When a question needs a bigger model, only the question travels, with names, numbers and addresses masked. The real values stay on the box and are put back into the answer there.

Always stays on the box
  • Your memory: everything it learns about you
  • Your chats, including imported history
  • Your files and their index
  • The real names, numbers and addresses
Can leave, filtered
  • A single question that needs a frontier model
  • Sent to the provider you picked, nobody else
  • Personal details replaced with placeholders, like [PERSON_1]

Not 100% private, and we won't pretend otherwise. Minimum exposure, by design, and no lock-in.
