Exposure, explained

What leaves
your desk.

Short answer: as little as possible, and never the things that make you you.

inteligentia is local-first, not air-gapped. Your agent lives on the box and does most of its work there. When a task truly needs a frontier model, the box sends a filtered request to the provider you chose. Here is every step.

The path of one request

01Ask

You message your agent on Telegram or another chat app. The box on your desk picks it up. We are not in the middle.

02Box first

The on-device model and the index of your files try to answer. Most daily questions end here.

03Filter

If it needs more horsepower, the filter masks names, numbers, addresses and IDs, and drops context the task doesn't need.

04Cloud

The thin, masked slice goes to your provider (OpenAI Codex or OpenRouter) on your own account.

05Put back

The answer returns, the real details are put back in locally, and the conversation is saved on the box.

Always stays with you

On the box

  • Your agent and everything it remembers about you
  • Every chat, past and present, including the history you import from ChatGPT or Claude
  • Your files and the search index built from them
  • The real names, numbers and addresses the filter masked
  • The answers you got back from the cloud, once they arrive
Can leave, filtered

To your provider

  • The masked text of a request that needs a frontier model
  • Only the context that task needs (the clause in question, not the whole contract)
  • Nothing goes to inteligentia. We are not in the middle.

Want zero exposure for a while? Disconnect the cloud brain. The agent keeps working on the box, just with a smaller brain.

What we won't pretend

  • It is not 100% private. When the agent asks a cloud model for help, filtered text leaves your desk. That's the trade for frontier-level answers, and it's your choice.
  • The filter is automatic, not magic. It catches the usual suspects (names, IDs, account numbers, phone numbers, emails, addresses). Unusual details written in free text can slip through, so keep anything truly secret in local-only conversations.
  • Once text reaches a provider, their policy applies. Check your provider's data settings. With OpenRouter, the request is served by the model host you pick.
Open core

Built on open source

The agent at the heart of the box is built on Hermes Agent, an open-source (MIT) project by Nous Research. We package, tune and look after it so you never touch a terminal. The core stays inspectable, and you're never locked in to us either.
